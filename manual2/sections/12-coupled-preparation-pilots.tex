\section{Coupled H/D/T Preparations}
\label{manual2:coupled-preparation-pilots}
\paragraph{Setup.}
What changes when an electron is added to the same isotope inventory under the
coupled recurrence law? The six preparations have $Z=1$, $A=1,2,3$ and
$E=0,1$, hence $N=A-1$ and charge $1-E$. Bare means $E=0$; for general $Z$,
a singly ionized preparation instead has $E=Z-1$. The labels H, D and T select
these inventories. Physical nuclear and electronic state correspondence
remains an additional application map.

The constituent templates, directed contact ring, orientation kernel, pacing
and response law are those of \secref{manual2:all-element-campaign}. Each
pilot executes one inner round and a separate outer return. The boundary is
fixed; this profile selects no external forcing or sweep term. Completed
contacts update later rounds, while the full directional histories remain
available. A single constituent has no artificial self-contact. The
application choices and stopping rule are fixed before comparison targets are
read. The new protocol uses the admitted constructions of AOD commit
\texttt{8c3149b}; earlier experiments retain their original source bindings.

\paragraph{Specialization.}
The bare/neutral contrasts hold isotope inventory, templates, law and round
horizon fixed. Adding the electron changes the boundary, contacts and terminal
window as well as the inventory. It adds one inner reclosure; the outer cuts
change by $779$, $44$ and $44$ for H, D and T. These are effects of the stated
preparation intervention, not an isolated physical electron-coupling effect.

A second H-neutral round exercises feedback. Its 16 exact paths give
$\mathbb{E}[J]=0$, $\mathbb{E}[C]=18$,
$\mathbb{E}[k_o]=20593/2$ and $n_i=4$, with
$\operatorname{Var}(J)=162$, $\operatorname{Var}(k_o)=301401/4$ and
$\operatorname{Cov}(J,k_o)=4941/2$. The shared histories therefore cannot be
replaced by independent current and timing marginals. The M3 finite-law map
of \mainequation{eq:m3-finite-law} propagates the full joint distribution;
direct path summation independently checks its covariance.

Each of the seven finite domains also retains all 32 declared seed runs.
Their maximum path-frequency discrepancies range from $1/32$ to $3/22$ and
satisfy the predeclared finite-sample diagnostic in the protocol. This is a
reproducible sampler check; a deterministic seed list does not itself acquire
an independent-sampling confidence guarantee.

For H neutral, four first-round histories reduce to two groups of two under
the retained response, directional RCD, cut, round and per-wave return counts.
Within each group, the complete joint law of the next reduced state and listed
readouts agrees. Equal burden alone fails this comparison. The result covers
one further step of this two-constituent law, not arbitrary future horizons
or scale changes; the fine histories remain stored.

A separate cancellation control begins with signed current zero and burden
18. Its declared compatible-support cost retains 9 and exports the opposing
9 through a certified route. Reserved, pending and arrived ownership remain
distinct. The retained current becomes 9, changing the next orientation
probabilities from $(1/2,1/2)$ to $(10/11,1/11)$. From the same cut and forced
orientation, pacing changes from 70 to 79 and the next return from 14295 to
14844. This diagnostic load/cost bridge demonstrates operative availability;
it is not a nuclear loss law. A further native control starts at equal current
zero with pressures 1 and 2: the same attention 1 produces currents 1 and
$1/2$. Present net current alone is therefore insufficient state.

The reference extension retains all 118 selected element identities as 236
bare/neutral preparation slots. Its first tranche executes the six reference
configurations at $Z=1,2,3$ under the same one-round law; 230 remain prepared
for execution. This tranche overlaps the H/D/T pilots and is counted separately.
The earlier 135 neutral configurations remain a distinct historical campaign.
No slot count is a count of completed physical predictions.

\paragraph{Falsification.}
Removing the passive observer or resuming the authenticated full state and RNG
after the first round leaves the trajectory and final RNG unchanged. A matched
H-neutral path has outer cut 10571 with feedback and 9473 with feedback disabled,
after a common first round; its respective path masses $5/44$ and $1/16$ forbid
calling this fixed-path contrast an unconditional sampling effect. Relabelling
constituent names preserves corresponding events and outputs.
Target mutation must leave frozen native outputs unchanged. An altered window,
current or population invalidates its cached observation.

A ring adjacency or scalar cross term cannot supply the missing FRG gate,
path/bin and value-map witness. An observed computational horizon is not
physical stability, decay or survival censoring. These limits apply to the
declared preparation, dynamics and readout conjunction; they neither suppress
its exact native results nor convert implementation checks into empirical
agreement.
