\subsection{Data support and record classes}\label{manual2:data-support}
Declare the input distinctions and their induced relations before a calculation,
report map or residual. The simulation data card inherited from the bound AOD
Manual is
\begin{equation}
(D,C_{\mathrm{support}},M_{\AO},\Pi_{\mathrm{report}},
\sigma_{\mathrm{input}},\epsilon_{\mathrm{report}},\mathrm{src}).
\label{eq:manual2-simulation-data-card}
\end{equation}
Here \(D\) names the input data, \(C_{\mathrm{support}}\) its support class,
\(M_{\AO}\) the calculation or map, \(\Pi_{\mathrm{report}}\) the reported
coordinate, \(\sigma_{\mathrm{input}}\) the input uncertainty where applicable,
\(\epsilon_{\mathrm{report}}\) the residual coordinate, and \(\mathrm{src}\)
the source record. Exact arithmetic of an input does not determine its physical
uncertainty. A source record can be declared before a model-to-target map exists.

Record form, scientific claim and data support have distinct roles. An exact
fixture stores its comparator and audit. A measured target record stores a
quantity, uncertainty, coordinate and citation; a calibration anchor stores a
convention value. An external comparison additionally binds the model's map
value and target residual. A benchmark manifest declares the required data and
report coordinate. Source-only acquisition records do not acquire an executed
O0 comparison merely by supplying a target. Static grouping arithmetic is not
automatically a D0 returned-current fixture.

The scientific record classes are native exact fixture, declared
model/simulation, metrology record and empirical comparison. Native fixtures
establish typed relations; models declare update and coupling laws; metrology
adds observation, calibration and units; empirical comparison adds sourced
targets and residuals. Each application states which of these operations it
performs. Admission, execution and empirical agreement remain separate evidence
claims. The G0--G3 and L0--L3 support classes of Manual I concern its declared
field-dynamics and lensing data; they are not additional Matter I maturity levels.
