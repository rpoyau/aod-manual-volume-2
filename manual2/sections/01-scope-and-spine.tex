\section{Imported Relations and Conditional Support Arithmetic}\label{sec:scope-and-spine}

\subsection{Distinction, motif and instance evidence}\label{subsec:sector-scope}
\paragraph{Setup.}
Main distinguishes Null sameness from committed finite events (\mainequation{eq:null-axiom}, \mainequation{eq:first-anchor}). The branch/hinge/counterbranch motif is
\[
M_{\varepsilon,m}=\varepsilon\,0_H(0_D)^m(-\varepsilon),
\]
with mandatory hinge and optional dwell (\mainequation{eq:hinge-grammar}). A faithful atomic edge changes one bit of \(Q_4\) (\mainequation{eq:atomic-h1}). These relations provide the motif template used by the visual projection.

\paragraph{Derivation.}
For a native instance, a declaration must bind its scope, lineage and construction:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf a\vdash(F,R,\partial_T R,\omega),\qquad B=(\partial_T F,\omega).
\label{eq:matter1-scope-binder}
\end{equation}
A certified closure additionally binds the retained-start return witness and at least two committed phase-bearing traversals (\mainequation{eq:closure-certificate}, \mainequation{eq:closure-phase-step-count}). The admitted motif record in \mainequation{eq:motif-admission} and traced admission in \mainequation{eq:key-proposed-admitted-trace} are prerequisites, not consequences of writing an elemental label.

\paragraph{Specialization.}
This reference supplies grouping, local tuples and conditional expressions for labelled rows. It does not supply the scope-bound instance evidence in \eqnref{eq:matter1-scope-binder}. Names identify the inherited distinctions or the declared reference records; they introduce no additional object or proof condition. Conversely, names cannot discharge the hypotheses of the construction they identify.

\paragraph{Falsification.}
A formatted support declaration represented as a successful certificate, or a plotted line represented as an executed edge without its trace, exceeds this profile.

\subsection{Support polynomial and the reflection-duration domain}\label{subsec:curling-curl-rd}
\paragraph{Setup.}
A support expression \(\Sigma=(s_1,\ldots,s_k;L)\), with clauses \(s_i=p_i{:}q_i\), is formal syntax (\mainequation{eq:support-expression}). The declared finite support model is
\begin{equation}
\Pi(p{:}q)=q^p+q,\qquad p,q\in\mathbb Z_{\ge1}.
\label{eq:matter1-pi-support}
\end{equation}
Its provenance is \mainequation{eq:curling-support-model} and \mainequation{app:rd:eq:path-support}. Admission of the proposed form remains
\begin{equation}
K_{\mathrm{prop}}\xrightarrow{\,HT(K;B)\,}K_{\mathrm{adm}}(B).
\label{eq:matter1-traced-admission}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Derivation.}
For an admitted instance in the domain of the inherited accessor,
\begin{equation}
RD_\gamma(B)=\Pi^-_\gamma(B)+H_{\mu,\gamma}(B)+\Pi^+_\gamma(B).
\label{eq:matter1-rd-general}
\end{equation}
Under equal branch supports and \(H_{\mu,\gamma}=1\),
\begin{equation}
RD^{(1)}_\gamma(B)=2\Pi(p{:}q)+1.
\label{eq:matter1-rd-single-hinge}
\end{equation}
These are \mainequation{eq:branch-hinge-rd}, \mainequation{eq:branch-hinge-rd-symmetric} and \mainequation{app:rd:eq:single-hinge-return}. Evaluating the right-hand expression numerically does not establish its accessor prerequisites.

The ordered count basis is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{curl}}=(3{:}3,\ 3{:}4,\ 1{:}2),
\label{eq:matter1-count-basis}

\paragraph{Falsification.}
The single-hinge expression cannot replace \eqnref{eq:matter1-rd-general} for unequal branches or a non-unit hinge. A declaration string cannot supply a missing construction witness or cycle-valued compression.
