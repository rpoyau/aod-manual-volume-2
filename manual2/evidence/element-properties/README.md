results are recorded separately; an entry in this table is not an executed
acceptance claim. Coverage records distinguish an absent reliable value from a
completed measurement. A missing physical law is a research outcome independent
of the catalog's software integrity.

preceding manifest. The static chart geometry, 118 labels and eight unfilled

Main receives no invented physical law. The downstream model declares and
executes generic native constructions, then exhibits distinct typed observation
maps on those histories. Native state correspondence, emitted-phase law,
energy/rest-mass observables, applicable realization uncertainty and a jointly
predictive multi-observable law remain the precise research dependencies. A

rebind and consumer checks before adoption here.

Bulk-material density, melting point, conductivity and related sample-dependent
properties remain outside this atomic/isotope program. The source catalog does
not convert a derived equivalent into an independently measured observable.


## Delivered data and scientific scope

The retained-source catalog has 500 entities and 4,354 rows: 1,067 numeric,
267 text, 2,756 unknown and 264 inapplicable. This includes 135 atomic masses,
source mass excesses and binding energies; derived neutron/proton separation
and alpha Q values; source beta Q, spin/parity, abundance and decay text;
H/D/T frequencies, the Cs defining anchor and four dated ENSDF moment/radius
values. Derived mass differences have no manufactured covariance uncertainty.

EP-2 is partial for electronic pilot properties. EP-5 and EP-6 are partial for
source acquisition: broad electronic states/energies, affinities, levels/lines,
standard atomic weights and response/moment/radius families remain unacquired.
The denied/cancelled network-acquisition attempt is recorded in the source
manifest. Offline ASD CSV and curated-transcription adapters are implemented.

EP-3/EP-4 deliver executable missing-premise analysis. The stipulated common-gain
family has an additional exact retrospective rejection under its declared H/D/T
ratio-envelope criterion. This is not a statistical significance statement, a
completed multi-observable physical law or a refutation of all AOD models.
Actual integrated check outcomes belong to the freshly executed receipts in the
implementation's checks directory, not to this source description.
