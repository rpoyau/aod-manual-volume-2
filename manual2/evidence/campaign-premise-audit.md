# Campaign premise audit

Bound producer: AOD PR43 `71cbcf7e637ababcde3b2e073fb4ceec4b1681b9`.
Consumer starting point: Matter I PR1 `bcbebc7684205263ac88557cfa1116e53fa34af3`.
This audit concerns the new finite application, not a universal limitation of AOD.

The source's fusion construction consumes admitted inputs and requires its own
outer witness (`sections/06_field.tex`, `eq:fusion-closure` and
`eq:fusion-branch-hinge-duration`). The campaign supplies distinct recurring
constituents, mapped scopes, genuine inner reclosures and the resulting outer
certificate. It does not infer an electronic or nuclear state from an inventory
label. The historical constructor recipe is instantiated with the separately
bound **current** native classes and admission predicates.

Native event and lineage names are local to each independently replayed
configuration. Cross-case joins use `(configuration_id, native_identity)`.
Sharing the same generic reference recipe across cases does not make those
independent reference instances one physical clock or synchronize their windows.

The new orientation, pacing and lag-to-reflection rules are explicit application
choices. Main's downstream continuation model permits declared rational weights
on admitted support; it does not uniquely select these weights for an atom.
The native API checks the constructed objects and operands. A passing check is
therefore evidence for the declared construction or execution within its scope.

No physical half-life, emission gain, per-isotope fitted rate or loss capacity is
introduced to fill these gaps. The implementation continues the independent
construction, stochastic execution, conservation, historical reduction, compact
display and reproduction work. The comparison records preserve the target's
quantity and status while the dependent physical prediction remains absent.

Hydrogen remains retrospective. Table IV's individual SAO result, Table VI's
NBS result and the published combined mean retain their own corrections and
uncertainties. Individual Lv event delays remain distinct from ensemble
half-life. Overlapping experimental records are not averaged as independent
measurements. These separations prevent an incomplete map from acquiring an
empirical agreement claim through a reporting convention.
