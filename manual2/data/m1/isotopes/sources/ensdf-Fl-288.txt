The 288 Fl nuclide has been identified by Dubna-Livermore-ORNL collaboration (2004Og12, also many other papers from this
   group), and later independently by the GSI group using TASCA separator (2012Ho12). A total of 27 EVR-α-SF correlated decay
   chains have been reported. Additionally, 288 Fl is also produced as alpha-daughter of 292 Lv, for which ten EVR-α-α-SF correlated
   decay chains have been reported (see 292 Lv Adopted Levels).

Experiments at FLNR-JINR-Dubna, using U-400 cyclotron and Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS):
288 Fl nuclide produced directly in experiments using nuclear reactions at FLNR-JINR-Dubna, and UNILAC-GSI laboratories. The

     analyses of the experiments at Dubna were also done independently at the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL). The
     radioactive targets were fabricated at the Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL).

2017Ka66 (also 2014MoZV): three correlated events were reported in 248 Cm(48 Ca,4n),E=261.6 MeV reaction using RIKEN Linear
   Accelerator (RILAC) and gas-filled recoil ion separator (GARIS). Two EVR-α-α-SF correlated events and the following one
   tentative EVR-α-α-α-SF event were observed, all starting from 292 Lv, with production σ=3.1 pb +28−18. All three events
   produced 288 Fl. See 292 Lv Adopted Levels for details.

2014Ya33: 288 ,289 Fl produced in 244 Pu(48 Ca,xn),(48 Ca,4n) reaction, and separated in-flight using TASCA-COMPACT-GSI
     separator, with the detection of two atoms, one each assigned to 288 Fl and 289 Fl. The adsorption behavior of Fl and Cn isotopes
     was studied together with their homologues Pb, Hg and Rn in groups 14 and 12 using isothermal gas chromatography and
     thermochromatography techniques. The Fl atoms adsorbed on Au surface formed a metal-metal but indicated higher inertness
     compared to homologue Pb in the group. It was concluded that Fl is a volatile metal, the least reactive one in group 14, and not as
     inert as a noble gas.
