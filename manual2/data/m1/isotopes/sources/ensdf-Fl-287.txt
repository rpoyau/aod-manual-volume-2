287 Fl has been observed as the product of the 244 Pu(48 Ca,5n) reaction at JINR (2004OG07) and the 242 Pu(48 Ca,3n) reaction at

     JINR (2004OG12) and LBNL (2009ST21); as the α-decay daughter of 291 Lv at JINR (2004OG07,2006OG05) and GSI
     (2016HO09 reassigns one chain tentatively assigned to 293 Lv in 2012HO12 to 291 Lv); and in a gas-phase chemistry experiment at
     JINR (2010EI01). Chains of genetically linked nuclei were identified based on the observation of time- and position-correlated α
     decays, terminated by spontaneous fission. 287 Fl was identified as a member of these chains based on the comparison of the
     observed decay properties of these chains with decay properties of known nuclei.
Other: 1999OG07 identifies two decay chains originating with 287 Fl, but these results have not been reproduced.
Half-lives, branching ratios, and α-decay energies in this evaluation have been computed from the individual events listed in the
     references above. Half-life uncertainties have been computed according to the method of 1984SC13. An additional 10 keV
     systematic uncertainty is assumed for the α-decay energies, which is added in quadrature to the averaged statistical uncertainty.
