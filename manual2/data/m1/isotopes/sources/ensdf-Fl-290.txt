2004Og10: reinterpretation of one long-lived decay chain observed by 1999Og10 in 244 Pu(48 Ca,xn),E=236 MeV (E*=35 MeV) at
    Dubna, and tentatively assigned to the decay of 289 Fl formed via 3n-channel, as possibly originating from the decay of 290 Fl via
    the 2n-channel. The decay chain would be: 290 Fl → 286 Cn → 282 Ds → 278 Hs (decays by SF). The cross-section for this event
    was ≈0.2 pb. This event was not observed in later experiments done at energies of E*=41-53 MeV (2000Og05,2000Og07), as the
    α particle energy of the first decay was ≈0.1 MeV less than that attributed to 289 Fl in the later work. A 3-min α-SF event
    observed in 1999Og07 at E*=32.6 MeV for 290 Fl and assigned to 287 Fl was not seen by 2004Og10.
2016Ho09: further discussion of the event assigned to 289 Fl in 1999Og10, that the observed decay chain did not fit from energy
    point of view to any of the decay chains with N-Z ≤61, however it fitted well with the systematics if 290 Fl, instead of 289 Fl, was
    produced via 2n-channel in the reaction studied by 1999Og10. 2016Ho09 further surmise that decay chain 290 Fl → 286 Cn →
    282 Ds → 278 Hs (decays by SF), as proposed earlier in 2004Og10, would be less likely as the SF half-life of 11.5 min +550−52 for

    the decay of 278 Hs would be too long to be realistic. Thus the authors propose rare decay of 290 Fl by ε mode to 290 Nh, followed
    by 290 Nh → 286 Rg → 282 Mt → 278 Bh (decays by SF). For details see sections 4.7 and 5.1.2 in the text and Fig. 2 decay
    scheme.
2017Ka66: tentative assignment of 290 Fl from decay of 294 Lv produced in 248 Cm(48 Ca,2n),E=261.6 MeV (E* =41.3 MeV reaction
    using RIKEN Linear Accelerator (RILAC) and gas-filled recoil ion separator (GARIS). One EVR-α-α-SF correlated event (chain
    #4) was interpreted in three ways, one being 294 Lv → 290 Fl → 286 Cn decay chain, while the other two involved (48 Ca,3n)
    channel producing 293 Lv → 289 Fl → 285 Cn, and 293 Lv → 289 Fl → 285 Cn → 281 Ds chains. See details of three interpretations
    of chain #4 in 294 Lv Adopted Levels dataset.
