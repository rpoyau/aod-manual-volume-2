EVR=Evaporation Residues.

The 292 Lv nuclide has been identified by Dubna-Livermore-ORNL collaboration (2004Og12, also many other papers from this
   group), and later independently by the GSI group (2012Ho12). A total of ten EVR-α-α-SF correlated decay chains have been
   reported so far. The 292 Lv nuclide decays 100% (2016Ho09) by α to 288 Fl. 2017Ka66 (also 2014MoZV) reported observation of
   three correlated events at GARIS-RIKEN facility, one with a tentative identification of EVR-α-α-α-SF event starting from 292 Lv
   and ending in 280 Ds, which decays by SF. No information is yet available from x-ray observation or chemical procedures.

Experiments at FLNR-JINR-Dubna, using Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS):
2004Og12, 2004OgZZ (also 2005Og03,2007Og05,2009Og07,2011Og07): 292 Lv produced and identified in 248 Cm(48 Ca,4n), E=247
   MeV, excitation energy of 36.8-41.1 MeV for the compound nucleus 296 Lv. Experiments carried out using U400 cyclotron and
   Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS) at FLNR-JINR-Dubna facility. Identification of mass number and nuclide was made on
   the basis of excitation functions, and detecting correlated events between evaporation residues (EVR), alpha particles and SF
   decays. A total of six decay chains were assigned to 292 Lv, but in one of these events EVR was missing, and in two events, one of
   the alphas escaped detection, as summarized below. Measured cross sections, EVR-α-α-SF correlations, Eα, half-lives. Note that an
   earlier assignment of one EVR-multiple α-SF correlated decay chain assigned to the 292 Lv nuclide was by the Dubna-Livermore
   collaboration in 2001Og01 and 2002Og09 (also 1999Og10,2000Og07, 2001Og06,2001Og11,2002Og03,2002Og09,2002Og13) by
   interpreting 4n-channel in 248 Cm(48 Ca,xn)296 Lv*,E(48 Ca)=240 MeV reaction, and compound nucleus excitation energy of
   30.4-35.8 MeV. Later this decay chain was assigned by 2004Og07 and 2004Og12 (also 2005Og03) to 3n-reaction channel, instead,
   thus leading to the discovery of the 293 Lv nuclide (see 293 Lv Adopted Levels).
