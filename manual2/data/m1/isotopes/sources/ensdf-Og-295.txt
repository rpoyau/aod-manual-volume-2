2016HO09 searched for production of element 120 using the 248 Cm(54 Cr,3n) reaction at GSI. Three correlated α decays were
   observed, followed by a high-energy event which could be spontaneous fission, were observed. The energy and decay time of the
   final α decay are in agreement with the properties of 291 Lv. However, an implantation event could not be identified
   unambiguously. The time between the final α decay and the potential spontaneous-fission event is also much longer than most other
   published data.
2017HE11 presents a reanalysis of the data in 2016HO09 and concludes that the three events are background events rather than a
   decay chain.
