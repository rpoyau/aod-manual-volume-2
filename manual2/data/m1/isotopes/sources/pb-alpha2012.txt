                                                    Abstract. For the first time a PbWO4 crystal was grown using ancient Roman lead and it was run as a
                                                    cryogenic detector. Thanks to the simultaneous and independent read-out of heat and scintillation light,
                                                    the detector was able to discriminate β/γ interactions with respect to α particles down to low energies.
                                                    New more stringent limits on the α decays of the lead isotopes are presented. In particular a limit of
                                                    T1/2 > 1.4·1020 y at a 90% C.L. was evaluated for the α decay of 204 Pb to 200 Hg.
