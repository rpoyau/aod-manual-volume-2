291 Lv has been produced in two experiments at JINR through the 245 Cm(48 Ca,2n) reaction using the U400 cyclotron coupled to

     DGFRS (2004Og07,2006Og05). An additional event that was tentatively identified as originating from 293 Lv in 2012HO12 was
     later reassigned to 291 Lv in 2016HO09. Four events have been observed in total. Candidate events were selected by the
     observation of chains of time- and position-correlated α decays, initiated with the implantation of evaporation residues in a
     position-sensitive focal-plane detector, and terminated by spontaneous fission. Positive identification of 291 Lv was based on
     comparison of the known properties of the daughter nuclei to those of the observed decay chains.
Half-lives, branching ratios, and α-decay energies in this evaluation have been computed from the individual events listed in the
     references above. Half-life uncertainties have been computed according to the method of 1984SC13. An additional 10 keV
     systematic uncertainty is assumed for the α-decay energies, which is added in quadrature to the averaged statistical uncertainty.
