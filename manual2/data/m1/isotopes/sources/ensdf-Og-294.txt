2006Og05, 2004Og10, 2004Og12 (also 2009Og07,2007Og05,2005Og03, 2005OgZZ,2004Og05,2003OgZZ,2002OgZX): discovery of
     Z=118 (Og) element through the identification of 294 Og nuclide decaying by three successive α-decay chains: 290 Lv, 286 Fl and
     282 Cn, which decays by SF. 294 Og from 249 Cf(48 Ca,3n),E=251 MeV; >98% enriched 249 Cf rotating target. The compound

     nucleus 297 Og formed at 32.1-36.6 MeV. Facility: U400 cyclotron with the Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS) of the
     Flerov Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions (FLNR), Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna (JINR), in collaboration with
     LLNL, USA. Evaporation residues recoiling from the target were separated by the DGFRS in flight from 48 Ca beam ions, transfer
     reaction products and scattered particles. Multiwire proportional counter for TOF measurement; semiconductor focal-plane detector
     array with 12 vertical position-sensitive strips surrounded by eight side detectors without position sensitivity. The α particles were
     measured in the range of 9.9 MeV to 12.0 MeV. FWHM=70-120 keV for α particles absorbed in the focal-plane detector, 280-410
     keV for α particles that escaped the focal-plane detector and were registered by the side detector.
History of three events observed: two correlated decay chains detected in the experiment at E(48 Ca=251 MeV) reported by
     2006Og05, and one correlated decay chain in an earlier experiment (2004Og10) at E(48 Ca=245 MeV). 2004Mo15 pointed out that
     the mass assignment in their work may be off by one unit. 2005St38 analyzed probability of observing a random event in their
     work.

2012Og06 (also 2011Og07): 249 Cf(48 Ca,3n),E=251.7 MeV; 249 Cf from decay of 249 Bk target; rotating 249 Bk radioactive target.
   249 Bk radioactive target was produced in neutron irradiation of Cm and Am targets in high flux isotope reactor at ORNL. The

   chemically purified 12.7 mg of 249 Bk (about half of the total 249 Bk material produced at ORNL) containing 0.51 mg of 249 Cf
   with no other detectable impurities was shipped to JINR. Six targets were prepared at RIAR facility in Russia, each target with a
   thickness of 0.33 mg/cm2 deposited on 0.72 mg/cm2 thick Ti foils, and mounted on a disk that was rotated at 1700 rpm
   perpendicular to the beam direction. U-400 cyclotron with the Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS) of the Flerov Laboratory
   of Nuclear Reactions, Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna (JINR). Evaporation residues passing through the DGFRS
   separator registered by time-of-flight system. Focal plane detector system: two 6x6 cm2 Si detectors, each of 16 strips with position
   sensitivity in the vertical direction. These detectors were surrounded by six similar 6x6 cm2 Si side detectors without position
   sensitivity. Behind the focal plane detectors, two veto Si detectors, similar to the side detectors, were mounted for detecting or
   rejecting signals from high-energy long-range charged particles produced in direct reactions of 48 Ca with the DGFRS materials.

2018Br13: E(48 Ca)=252,258 MeV beam from U400 cyclotron of JINR-Dubna was incident on a mixed Cf target (50.6% 249 Cf,
   13.1% 250 Cf and 36.3% 252 Cf) obtained from >30 year old 252 Cf samples at ORNL. The Cf target was fabricated at ORNL as 12
   segments, each with a layer of 309 µg/cm2 thick Cf, containing a total of 1 mg of mixed Cf source. Fragments were separated by
   Dubna Gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS), and ions were tagged with time-of-flight (TOF) in two pentane filled multi-wire
   proportional counters (MWPC). Focal plane detector array for the detection of α particles, designed and assembled at ORNL, was a
   double-sided silicon detector (DSSD) consisting of 48 horizontal strips on front side and 128 vertical strips on the back side for
   position resolution. The escaped α particles were detected in a box of six Si detectors surrounding the focal plane detector. A veto
   detector was placed behind the focal plane detector to reject light particles. Calibration of the detector system and assessment of
   target condition was carried out using Yb(48 Ca,xn) reaction producing α-decaying isotopes of 216 Th to 220 Th. Measured recoil-α
   correlated events, and production cross section. Deduced one event assigned to the decay of 294 Og through 294 Og → 290 Lv →
   286 Fl. Reevaluated T        294 Og decay, and Eα emitted in this decay by combining the previous data for four observed events
