\section{Exact Parity, Role and Occupancy Comparators}\label{manual2:app:coordinate-phase-delta3}

\subsection{Integer comparators}\label{manual2:app:delta3-form}
On each complete reference group \(\mathsf C_{2m}\), \(m=3,\ldots,11\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item Exactly \(m\) seats have odd local parity and \(m\) even local parity. Against the comparator \(\widehat O_m=m\),
\[
\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_{\mathrm{parity},m}=\widehat O_m-O_{\mathrm{even},m}=0.
\]
Parity does not determine the assigned operational class.
\item The declared role projection has \(m-1\) first-branch seats, one hinge seat, \(m-1\) counterbranch seats and one boundary seat. Thus
\[
\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_{\mathrm{branch},m}=(m-1)-(m-1)=0.
\]
These are counts of visual roles, not traversed edge events.
\item The terminal group has 14 labelled slots and capacity 22:
\[
\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_{\mathsf C_{22}}=14-22=-8.
\]
The difference counts eight unfilled reference slots and is not a retention or physical-cutoff residual.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Signed-magnitude record}\label{manual2:app:delta3-lock}
The comparator representation is
\[
\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_k=s_k^{(3)}m_k,\qquad
s_k^{(3)}\in\{-1,0,+1\},\quad m_k=|\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_k|.
\]
The two balanced comparisons give \(\delta_3=(0,0)\); the terminal occupancy comparison gives \(\delta_3=(-1,8)\). These values express the declared integer counts, not a native phase-cycle measurement.
