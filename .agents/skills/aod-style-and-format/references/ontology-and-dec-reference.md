## 2. Main Definitions 1–26 & Appendices A–J

1. **Curl** (`§3.1.1`, `App. B.1.2`): Returned closure through a hinge on its certified support.

3. **Pin-curl** (`§3.1.3`, `App. B.1.4`): A returned curl whose residue seats, delays, or fixes a hinge.

5. **Dion** (`§3.2`, `App. B.1.7`): Oriented two-position branch pattern around the retained monon hinge: $D_\alpha=(+1,-1),\;D_\Omega=(-1,+1)$.

10. **Region** (`§3.6.1`): Declared retained part of a field carrying current, closure, pressure, and returned-current orientation.

12. **Polychiral shell dressing** (`§3.6.4`): Field outer dressing carrying $\mathrm{A}/\Omega$ return orientation, polarization imbalance, return duration, pressure, and SADAR coupling.

### 3.3 Blocking Rule, Hinge Slide & Field Tunnelling (`Manual §§4.4–4.6`)

Slot $e_3$ may re-enter only as an admitted branch state $\sigma'\in\{-1,+1\}$.
2. **Hinge Slide Renormalization:** Declare the post-block slide pool $\operatorname{Slide}_\mu(B|_t)=\{s_1,\dots,s_n\}$ with weights $w'(s_i)$ and normalize **only after** the slide pool is declared:
   $$Z_{\mathrm{slide}}(B|_t)=\sum_{s_i\in\operatorname{Slide}_\mu(B|_t)}w(s_i;B|_t),\qquad P_{\mathrm{slide}}(s_i;B|_t)=\frac{w(s_i;B|_t)}{Z_{\mathrm{slide}}(B|_t)}.$$
